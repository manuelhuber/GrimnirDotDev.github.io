About

After getting laid off from my programming job, losing my work visa, and having to leave the US, I decided to take the chance and turn my lifelong love for games and building things into a new career as a solo indie dev.

I made Solitary Singleplayer Offline MMORPG because I have deep nostalgia for the time I spent playing World of Warcraft, starting with The Burning Crusade. But I no longer have the time or energy to play an MMO like a fulltime job, and after I got hooked on incremental games I decided that'd be a perfect genre blend for a management game about a raid guild.

SSOMMORPG is my first commercial release, after 11 years as a professional software engineer, including working on virtual reality for Oculus.
